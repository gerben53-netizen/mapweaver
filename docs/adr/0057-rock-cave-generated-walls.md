# ADR-0057 — Rock/Cave Generated Wall strategy

Accepted 3 October 2026. Additive to ADR-0051/0055. The immutable registry admits `generatorType: rock` with three required radial mass roles (`rock-large`, `rock-medium`, `rock-small`). Nine development PNGs provide three variants per role. No endpoint/corner/jamb/elongated role is necessary for the engineering benchmark.

A rock recipe requires `roughness` and `variation`, both 0–1, alongside the existing ID/revision/uint32 seed. Reserved root fields remain zero; masonry Regularity is rejected. Existing Living Root/masonry recipes, PNGs and strategies are unchanged. Format 12 gains optional rock recipe fields, validated centrally in browser and service. Only native Wall geometry, physical reference, recipe and existing Opening objects persist; generated masses/cache surfaces never become scene stamps or document data.

The strategy places dense overlapping opaque rock cores, bounded edge masses and independently scattered surface masses. This is coverage packing, not construction courses: surface masses bridge the internal coverage lattice, variants rotate freely, and the native route remains canonical. Core canvas diameter is 1.6 times local width per coverage column, with at most approximately one-foot column spacing. Wide walls add masses; square component aspect is preserved. Indexed seeded variation controls weighted choice, rotation, restrained center jitter and ±4% uniform scale. Roughness varies edge displacement and size. There is no Density control, mirroring, floor-texture strip or nominal-band clip.

Every component must provide a fully opaque centered disk of radius 0.39 canvas width, within a radial silhouette of at most 0.47 canvas width (up to one source-pixel antialias fringe, still inside safe bounds). Steps are 0.3 core diameters and core jitter is bounded. The opaque cores overlap even under allowed scale variation; final PNG tests sample the route/inner band at both export resolutions. Irregular PNG edges, not a rectangular strip, form the boundary. Closed seams and abrupt turns receive ordinary mass infill within a bounded node disk. Open ends terminate with inset rounded masses. Existing opening intervals split placement, and the shared wide opening-only clip prevents passage obstruction. Independent T/crossing/corridor walls remain independent native objects; no global graph.

Existing half-resolution editing previews, final cached surfaces (up to 2 samples/map pixel), 12,000-placement admission/actual caps, 4 Mi-pixel surface and 16 Mi-pixel source/retained-cache budgets remain in force. Rock admission invokes its bounded layout to reject impossible inputs before rendering. Large/complex walls may need splitting; no new worker or global cache architecture is introduced.

Cave Rock — DEV is available in local non-installer Wall material selection and clearly marked development. Installer choices hide it except an exact saved selection, retaining resource availability. No production material is introduced. Component PNGs are deterministic diagnostic SVG-derived fixtures, never Premium Cave Floor or other production artwork. `node scripts/create-rock-wall-development-components.mjs` reproduces them; `node scripts/export-rock-cave-wall-spec.mjs` creates the standalone nine-file Natural Cave Rock commission.

Editor/history/autosave/SQLite/archive/PNG reuse existing native paths. Campfire receives ordinary wall/opening semantics from the current canonical v2 baseline; no cave-specific contract entity, renderer field or schema change is introduced. The linked Campfire contract is read-only.
